## 🔬 What I’m exploring

> How can statistical and machine-learning methods help us learn more reliably from longitudinal, heterogeneous health data?

I develop statistical and machine-learning methods for complex health data. My current interests center on **longitudinal EHR analysis, multimodal learning, and rigorous statistical modeling**—connecting flexible representations with calibrated prediction, interpretable analysis, and reproducible clinical research.

| 🧠 01 · Model | 🕰️ 02 · Follow | 🧩 03 · Integrate |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Develop statistically principled learning methods with uncertainty and evaluation built in. | Learn from patient histories as evolving trajectories—not isolated snapshots. | Combine structured records, clinical text, medical images, and temporal signals. |
